Web Site Critiques

INFO 2450 - Project 1

Project Questions

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information?
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Jami Lin

Website URL: https://www.jamilin.com/

1. My first impression of this site was that it focuses on spirituality and wellness topics, especially astrology and feng shui. The site feels very content-driven and gives the impression that it is meant to educate and inform rather than just advertise something.

2. The site organizes information by grouping content into different categories based on topics. Instead of guiding users through one main path, the site allows visitors to choose what type of content they want to explore.

3. Overall, the site structure makes sense once you spend some time on it. However, at first it can feel a little overwhelming because there are many different links and sections available right away.

4. The graphics and layout generally match the theme of the site and help support the overall message. That said, the large amount of content on the page can make it feel busy and slightly harder to focus on one thing at a time.

5. The home page loaded in a reasonable amount of time when I accessed the site. There were no major delays, and the content appeared quickly enough to keep my attention.

6. The text color and size were readable throughout the site. The contrast between the text and background made it easy to read without needing to zoom in or adjust settings.

7. The site includes navigation tools that allow users to move between different sections and pages. The navigation works well overall, although simplifying it could help new visitors find information more easily.

8. The site uses a consistent color scheme and overall style across its pages. This consistency helps the website feel more organized and visually connected.

9. When the browser window is resized to a smaller size, the site remains functional and readable. While some sections become more compact, the content is still accessible and usable.

10. One thing the site does particularly well is offering a unique combination of astrology, feng shui, and personal guidance content. This makes the site stand out compared to more general wellness or lifestyle websites.
